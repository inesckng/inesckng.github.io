Privacy policy — Backtest Strategy

Effective: 16 October 2026.

Use. Google identity information confirms the sender's email address. Gmail sending permission delivers reports to the configured recipient; it does not provide inbox-reading access.

Storage and sharing. Authorization credentials and the verified sender email are stored locally on the owner's computer. Sender, recipient, subject, message and report images are transmitted to Google over HTTPS for delivery to the recipient. Google account data is not sold or used for advertising or AI training.

Retention and deletion. Local credentials remain until deleted or replaced. Revoke access in Google Account connections and delete local authorization credentials to disconnect the app. Previously sent messages remain until removed from the relevant email accounts.

Limited Use. Use and transfer of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact. Privacy questions can be sent to the operator through this information site's repository issue tracker.
